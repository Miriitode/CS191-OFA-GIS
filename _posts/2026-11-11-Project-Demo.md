This demo shows the core functionalities of the web application:

- Display AFAs as pins on the map
- Hovering on a map pin shows simple information of a given AFA
- Clicking on a map pin shows more details of the AFA on the sidebar
- Filter shown AFAs on the map with the following filering options:
  - Academic Year and Semester
  - Date Range
  - More filtering options to come (Type of AFA, AFAs by department, etc.)
